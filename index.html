Why AI + Cyber Security?

In the past, I explored the fundamentals of computer networking and defensive cybersecurity — from packet tracing in Wireshark to examining OSI layer protocols. Rather than keeping it as separate theory, my goal as an AIML undergraduate is to unite Deep Learning architectures with network telemetry.

Traditional rule-based firewalls struggle against polymorphic payloads and zero-day threats. By deploying neural classifiers directly on live packet streams, we can detect anomalous behavior in micro-seconds.

Phase 1: Protocol & Telemetry Foundations
Deep mastery of TCP/IP handshakes, DNS query telemetry, subnetting, and raw socket programming.
Phase 2: Deep Learning on Network Datasets
Training neural networks on labeled network intrusion datasets (NSL-KDD, CICIDS2017) to classify multi-vector attacks.
Phase 3: Real-Time Anomaly Detection Engine (Upcoming)
Deploying an end-to-end open-source intrusion detector powered by PyTorch and packet stream telemetry.
